Money follow

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This app has no company, server, or account behind it — it's a personal finance tracker that runs entirely on your device.

The short version

Money follow stores everything — transactions, wallets, goals, PIN, settings — only on this device, in your browser's local storage. There is no account, no login, no backend server that your financial data is ever sent to. Nothing is sold, shared, or monetized.

What's stored, and where

All app data (transactions, wallet balances, Vault goals, Khata entries, recurring rules, receipt photos) lives in your browser's IndexedDB. Settings and your PIN's hash live in localStorage. Both are sandboxed to this site and this device/browser — clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, permanently deletes them.

Your PIN itself is never stored — only a one-way hash of it, so even inspecting the storage doesn't reveal the PIN.

Camera use

The camera is used for two things, both entirely on-device: scanning a UPI QR code to pre-fill a payment, and optionally attaching a photo of a receipt to an expense. Scanned QR/payment details are never saved anywhere — they're used only to build the one-time payment link and then discarded. Receipt photos are compressed and stored locally like any other transaction data, and are included only if you attach one.

Share-to-Add (off by default)

If you turn this on in Settings, you can share a bank/UPI notification text into the app to pre-fill an expense. The text is parsed entirely on-device and is never sent anywhere or written to storage — it exists only in memory for the moment it takes to show you the pre-filled entry. Messages that look like they contain an OTP or verification/security code are always ignored outright, whether or not this feature is on.

Notifications

Notification permission, if you grant it, is used only to show you reminders and alerts you've enabled in Settings (e.g. a budget warning, a recurring bill due). These are triggered locally by the app while it's open or briefly in the background — nothing about your finances is transmitted to any notification server.

Backups

Exporting a backup creates a JSON file on your device that you choose where to save or share. You can optionally protect it with a password — this encrypts the file (AES-256) using your browser's built-in Web Crypto API before it's saved, entirely on-device. If you set a password, remember it: it cannot be recovered by anyone, including by re-installing or contacting anyone, since there is no account system.

Third-party code this app loads

To function, the app loads a few open-source libraries from public CDNs (Dexie, Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, jsPDF, SheetJS) and a font from Google Fonts. Loading any file from the internet inherently shares your IP address and basic request metadata with that server, the same way loading any website does — none of these requests include your financial data.

This app itself does not use analytics, trackers, or advertising of any kind.

UPI payments

When you pay via a UPI link (scanned or typed), the app hands the payment details to your phone's own UPI app to complete — Money follow is not a party to that transaction and has no visibility into your bank or UPI credentials at any point.

Your control

You can export or erase all your data at any time from Settings. Erasing data is immediate and permanent — there is no copy anywhere else to restore from, since none is ever kept off this device.
